Stay on budget, grow a cactus

Cactolo is a monthly budget you type in by hand, in a few seconds. Your month starts on payday, not on the 1st. There is no bank to connect and everything stays on your phone. Every month you stay on budget, your cactus grows.

Budget for two

One of you invites the other with a link or a QR code. Shared expenses are encrypted on the phone before they leave it, and the key stays on your two phones: the server that passes them along only sees data it cannot read. Leaving the shared budget deletes it from the server.

Stays on your phone

No account, no bank link, no ads, no tracking. Your expenses, your budget and your settings stay on your phone.

Questions

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You type each expense yourself, it takes a few seconds. You never give a bank login.

Why does the month start on payday?

Because that is when your money comes in. Paid on the 25th? Your budget runs from the 25th to the 24th.

What if I go over budget?

Your cactus looks upset for a while, then you catch up. It never loses what it grew.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. One Pro covers both people in a budget for two.
