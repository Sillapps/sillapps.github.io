SILLAPPS

App name: Shubh Samay

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shubh Samay handles information when you use the mobile application. Shubh Samay shows traditional Hindu time divisions (choghadiya, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Abhijit and Brahma muhurat) calculated from the sunrise and sunset at a location you choose. It is an informational tool and makes no predictions.

Data the developer collects

The developer of Shubh Samay does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account and no analytics SDK. All timings are calculated on your device; the app does not download them.

Location

Using your current location is optional. If you tap “Use my current location” and grant permission, the app reads one approximate (coarse) position from your device, stores the coordinates on your device to calculate sunrise and sunset, and names it after the nearest city from a list built into the app. The position is never sent anywhere by the app. You can instead pick a city from the list or type coordinates, in which case no location permission is used.

Data stored on your device

The chosen place, your preferences (language, time format, alert settings), whether Shubh Samay Pro or its free trial is active, and simple usage counters (the first and last day you opened the app, how many days you used it, when a Pro reminder or the rating prompt was last shown) are stored only on your device. They are used to decide when to show the ad banner, a Pro reminder or the rating prompt, and never leave the device.

Advertising

The free version shows one banner ad on the main screen (not on the first day of use, and never for Pro buyers). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve, measure and personalize ads, and to prevent fraud, as described in Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and “Ad privacy choices” in Settings lets you change it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

Shubh Samay Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned. The 7-day free trial of widgets and alerts is tracked on your device only.

Notifications and widgets

If you enable alerts (Pro or trial), notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widgets read timings stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies and your Google account and device settings.

Children's privacy

Shubh Samay is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
