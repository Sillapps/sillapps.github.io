FUEL TOMORROW WA

Privacy Policy

SILLAPPS

App name: Fuel Tomorrow WA

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Fuel Tomorrow WA handles information when you use the mobile application. Fuel Tomorrow WA shows Western Australia fuel prices for today and tomorrow, as published by FuelWatch (Government of Western Australia), and tells you whether to fill up now or wait. Fuel Tomorrow WA is an informational tool; it is not an official source and is not affiliated with FuelWatch or the Government of Western Australia.

Data collected

Fuel Tomorrow WA does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The suburbs you save, your fuel type, your favourite stations and your preferences (alert on or off, members-only sites) are stored only on your device. The app also stores a local copy of the prices it has read, simple usage counters (such as the number of days the app was opened, used to space out reminders and the rating prompt), whether the free Pro trial was started, and whether Fuel Tomorrow Pro is unlocked.

The app turns off Android's app backup, so none of this is copied to your Google account.

Location

Location is optional. If you tap “Use my location” or “Show distances”, the app asks Android for your approximate location. It is used on your device only, to find the nearest suburb in the app's built-in list and to show distances to stations. Your position is never sent to the developer or to FuelWatch; only the name of the suburb you choose is part of the price request described below. The last approximate position is kept on your device so distances can be shown the next time you open the app.

Network requests

The app downloads prices from the public FuelWatch RSS feed of the Government of Western Australia (https://www.fuelwatch.wa.gov.au/fuelwatch/fuelWatchRSS). Each request contains the fuel type, the suburb name, whether surrounding suburbs are included and the day (yesterday, today or tomorrow). It contains no identifier and no personal detail.

As with any internet request, FuelWatch can see your IP address; its processing is governed by the Government of Western Australia's own policies.

Purchases

Fuel Tomorrow Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned. The free trial is recorded on your device only.

Notifications, widget and background refresh

While Pro or the free trial is active, the app refreshes prices from FuelWatch in the background about once an hour, for the home screen widget, using the same request as above. If you turn on the alert, it also checks around 2:35pm (and every 15 minutes until 9pm if tomorrow's prices are late) and shows a local notification. The widget reads prices stored locally by the app. The app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating prompt; Google handles that prompt under its own policy. None of these features sends data to the developer.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. Cached prices older than two days are deleted automatically. You can remove saved suburbs and favourites in the app, or delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Fuel Tomorrow WA is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
