HORA BARATA

Privacy Policy

App name: Hora Barata (formerly Eco Claims Pocket)

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Versión en español: Política de privacidad

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Hora Barata handles information when you use the mobile application. Hora Barata shows the Spanish regulated electricity price (PVPC) hour by hour and helps you find the cheapest time to use electricity.

Data controller

The app is published by Maxime Silla Pellicer (Sillapps), sole trader, 19 Rue des Causses, 91940 Les Ulis, France. Contact: contact@sillapps.com.

Data collected

Hora Barata does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device: your preferences (language, taxes display, appliance duration, alerts on or off), a cache of recent electricity prices, whether Hora Barata Pro is unlocked (or a trial is active), and when the app last asked you to rate it.

Network requests

To download prices, the app sends anonymous requests to public open-data services of Red Eléctrica (apidatos.ree.es and api.esios.ree.es). These requests contain only the date requested. As with any internet request, those services can see your IP address; their processing is governed by Red Eléctrica's own policies.

Purchases

Hora Barata Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned.

Ratings and reviews

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's official In-App Review API. The rating dialog is shown and handled by Google Play: the developer does not receive your rating or review through the app, and any review you post is published on Google Play under your Google account, according to Google's policies.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro), notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widget reads prices stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Google Play

Downloading and updating the app, purchases and reviews go through Google Play, which is operated by Google under its own privacy policy. Google may provide the developer with aggregated statistics (such as installs and crash reports) through the Play Console; these do not identify you.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. In practice Sillapps does not process any personal data through the app, so there is nothing to access or delete on our side. If you write to us by email, we use your address and message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed to handle your request.

You can exercise these rights or ask any question at contact@sillapps.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France (cnil.fr) or the AEPD in Spain (aepd.es).

Children's privacy

Hora Barata is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: contact@sillapps.com
