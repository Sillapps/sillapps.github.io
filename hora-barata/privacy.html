HORA BARATA

Privacy Policy

SILLAPPS

App name: Hora Barata (formerly Eco Claims Pocket)

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Hora Barata handles information when you use the mobile application. Hora Barata shows the Spanish regulated electricity price (PVPC) hour by hour and helps you find the cheapest time to use electricity.

Data collected

Hora Barata does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device: your preferences (language, taxes display, appliance duration, alerts on or off), a cache of recent electricity prices, and whether Hora Barata Pro is unlocked.

Network requests

To download prices, the app sends anonymous requests to public open-data services of Red Eléctrica (apidatos.ree.es and api.esios.ree.es). These requests contain only the date requested. As with any internet request, those services can see your IP address; their processing is governed by Red Eléctrica's own policies.

Purchases

Hora Barata Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro), notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widget reads prices stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Hora Barata is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
