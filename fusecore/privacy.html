App name: FUSECORE: Idle Tower Defense

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FUSECORE handles information when you use the mobile application. FUSECORE is an idle tower defense game with local progression, offline earnings, simulated leagues against computer-controlled opponents, optional Google Play Games features (leaderboards and cloud save), optional in-app purchases, optional rewarded ads served through Google AdMob, and advertising privacy consent flows served through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Data stored on your device

FUSECORE stores your game data locally on your device, in encrypted form, so the game can function as expected. This local data may include progression, in-game currencies, your tower build and loadout, unlocked upgrades and talents, records, league standings, the time of your last session (used to compute offline earnings), language preference, and audio settings.

FUSECORE does not require an account for local progression and does not operate a first-party server or database. Opponents in leagues are simulated on your device; no other player's data is shown or collected through leagues.

Data processed by third-party services

FUSECORE uses the following Google services. They may collect and process limited technical data needed to operate them, which may include app instance or advertising identifiers, device model and operating system, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction events, crash and diagnostic data, performance information, transaction status, and fraud-prevention or security signals.

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage events (for example sessions, waves reached, screens viewed) used to understand how the game is played and to improve balancing.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and diagnostic information used to fix bugs and stability issues.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads game configuration (such as balancing values or events) without an app update.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — if you allow notifications, a device token is used to deliver notifications (for example when offline earnings are ready).
  • Google Play Games Services — if you sign in, your Play Games player identifier, display name and avatar, your scores (submitted to leaderboards) and, if enabled, a cloud backup of your game progress are processed by Google.
  • Google AdMob and Google UMP — deliver optional rewarded ads and record your advertising privacy choices.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases and restores entitlements.

Data processing performed by these providers is governed by their own privacy terms, including the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

How data is used

Local app data is used to save your game state and preferences on the device, and to compute offline earnings between sessions.

Analytics and crash data are used in aggregate to improve the game's stability, performance and balancing. They are not used to identify you personally and are not sold.

If rewarded ads are offered, advertising and consent providers may use relevant data to deliver ads, record and apply privacy choices, measure service performance and prevent abuse. Depending on your location and your consent choices, the app may request personalized or non-personalized ads. If you decline consent for personalized advertising where such a choice is required, the app is intended to use non-personalized ad requests.

If in-app purchases are offered, store providers may use relevant data to process purchases, restore entitlements, prevent fraud and operate billing systems.

Sharing your runs

FUSECORE lets you share a summary of a run (for example your build and the wave reached). Sharing only happens when you choose to, through your device's clipboard or share sheet; FUSECORE does not upload this content itself.

Your privacy choices

Where required by law, FUSECORE asks for your advertising privacy choices before initializing ad services. You can review or change these choices at any time from the in-app privacy settings when that option is available.

Google Play Games sign-in is optional. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in your device settings.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage through your device settings. Cloud saves stored through Google Play Games can be managed or deleted from your Google Play Games settings.

Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to its standard retention settings. You can request deletion of data associated with your app installation by contacting us at the address below.

Security

FUSECORE keeps core progression data on the device, encrypted at rest, and minimizes first-party remote data handling. However, no device, network, or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

FUSECORE is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app is not configured as a child-directed service under COPPA and does not knowingly collect personal data from children through a first-party account system or direct submission forms.

Changes to this privacy policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, data handling, or legal requirements change. Any update will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
