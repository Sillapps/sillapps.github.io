Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information. The app shows the payment dates of social benefits (for example Bolsa Família and INSS in Brazil, the Bienestar pensions in Mexico and SASSA grants in South Africa) from the official calendars published by each government. It is an informational tool: it is not an official source and is not affiliated with any government or agency.

Data you enter

The app never asks for a CPF, NIS, CURP, ID number, name on a card, bank account or any document number. To pick the right date it only needs the one piece of information the official calendar uses: the last digit of a number, a birth month, the first letter of a surname or a grant type. You can also give an optional nickname to each person you follow (for example "Mum").

These choices, your country, your alert preferences, local usage counters (first day of use, number of days the app was opened, when a reminder or rating prompt was shown) and whether Pro or its trial is active are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer.

Network requests

The app downloads the current payment calendars from https://sillapps.com/beneficios/calendars.json, a static file hosted on GitHub Pages. The request contains nothing about you. Links to official websites open in your browser.

Advertising

In the free version the app shows one banner ad on the main screen (not on the first day of use, never in notifications, the widget or the purchase screen). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and use the device advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve, measure and, where you allow it, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Buying Pro removes ads.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned. The free 7-day trial is stored on your device and needs no account or card.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro), notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widget reads dates stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can remove a person in the app, or delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
