The data controller for this app is Maxime Silla Pellicer (sole trader, France), who publishes it as Sillapps, 19 Rue des Causses, 91940 Les Ulis, France. Contact: contact@sillapps.com, for any question or request about your personal data.

What the app is

The app shows the payment dates of social benefits in Brazil, Mexico and South Africa from the official calendars published by each government. It is informational and unofficial: it does not represent any government entity and is not affiliated with any government or agency.

Data you enter, kept on your phone

The app never asks for a CPF, NIS, CURP, ID number, card, bank account, password or any document number. It only needs the one thing the official calendar uses: the last digit of a number, a birth month, the first letter of a surname or a grant group, plus an optional nickname for each person you follow.

These choices, your country, alert preferences, Pro and trial status, local usage counters (first day of use, days opened, when a reminder or rating prompt was shown) and a short local log of unexpected errors are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

Android backup: if backup is on in your phone settings, Android may copy these settings to your Google account so they come back after a reinstall. This is handled by Google under your account settings.

Network requests

The app downloads the payment calendars from https://sillapps.com/beneficios/calendars.json, a static file hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the host (GitHub, Inc., USA), which may keep technical logs for security. The request carries no identifier or data about you. Links to official websites open in your browser.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows one banner ad on the main screen (not on the first day of use; never in notifications, the widget or the purchase screen). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and share the device advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interaction data and diagnostics, to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud (see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and Settings > Ad privacy choices lets you change it. Elsewhere, ads are shown on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in funding a free app; you can limit personalized ads and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Buying Pro removes ads.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; the developer does not receive them. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro is owned. The free trial is stored on your device and needs no account or card.

Notifications and widget

Alerts are scheduled locally on your device and the home screen widget reads dates stored locally by the app. Neither sends data anywhere.

Contacting us

If you use "Report a mistake or contact us", your email app opens a message to contact@sillapps.com with the app version, the calendar version and the names of the programs you follow; you choose what to send. Emails are used only to answer you and are deleted when no longer needed.
