PLACA HOY

Privacy Policy

SILLAPPS

App name: Placa Hoy

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Placa Hoy handles information when you use the mobile application. Placa Hoy tells you whether a vehicle can drive on a given day under local driving restriction programs such as “pico y placa” in Colombia and Ecuador and “Hoy No Circula” in the Mexico City metropolitan area. Placa Hoy is an informational tool; it is not an official source and is not affiliated with any government or transit authority.

Data collected

Placa Hoy does not collect, store on a server, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

The vehicle details you enter (name, plate, city, vehicle type, fuel, verification sticker, permit dates) and your preferences (alerts on or off, alert times) are stored only on your device. The app also stores a local copy of the restriction rules, the last environmental contingency status it checked, and whether Placa Hoy Pro is unlocked.

Network requests

The app makes two kinds of anonymous requests, which never include your plate or any vehicle detail:

1. It downloads the current restriction rules from https://sillapps.com/placa-hoy/rules.json, a static file hosted on GitHub Pages.

2. For vehicles in the Mexico City area, it downloads the public air quality page of the Mexico City government (https://aire.cdmx.gob.mx/default.php) to check whether an environmental contingency is active.

As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address; their processing is governed by their own policies (GitHub and the Government of Mexico City).

Purchases

Placa Hoy Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro), notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The home screen widget reads answers stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete a vehicle in the app, or delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Placa Hoy is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
