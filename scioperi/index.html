Scioperi Oggi

SILLAPPS

Scioperi Oggi tells you whether a transport strike (trains, local transport, flights, ferries, taxis) or a general strike affects your Italian region today, tomorrow or this week, from the official strike list of the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. It is an independent app, not affiliated with the Ministry. This page provides the public legal entry for the app and supports app store compliance and listing requirements.
