Privacy Policy

SILLAPPS

App name: Scioperi Oggi

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Scioperi Oggi handles information when you use the mobile application. Scioperi Oggi shows scheduled strikes in Italy (trains, local public transport, air, maritime, taxis and general strikes) for the regions and sectors you choose, using the public strike list of the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. Scioperi Oggi is an informational tool; it is not an official source and is not affiliated with the Ministry, the Commissione di Garanzia or any transport operator.

Data the developer collects

The developer does not collect, store on a server, or sell personal data. There is no account and no analytics SDK.

Your areas (region and province), sector filters, alert settings, the local copy of the strike list, the start date of the free trial and whether Scioperi Oggi Pro is unlocked are stored only on your device.

Network requests

The app downloads the public strike list from the website of the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (https://scioperi.mit.gov.it: the RSS feed and the public strike table). These requests are anonymous and never include your areas or settings. As with any internet request, the Ministry's servers can see your IP address; their processing is governed by the Ministry's own policies.

Advertising

The free version shows a single banner ad on the main screen (never on the first day of use, never in notifications, the widget or the purchase screen). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where required by law (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland) through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Ads and privacy choices".

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process the device advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location), app interactions with ads and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Scioperi Oggi Pro removes ads entirely.

Purchases

Scioperi Oggi Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned. The 7-day free trial is recorded only on your device.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro or trial), notifications are scheduled locally on your device, and the app checks the public strike list in the background every few hours to tell you about newly announced strikes. The home screen widget reads data stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data anywhere.

Ratings

The app may occasionally show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Any rating or review is handled by Google Play; the app does not see it.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Scioperi Oggi is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
