Stelami · Android and iPhone

The birth chart of your crew

Stelami reads the birth chart of a group of friends: who makes the plans, who listens, who gets along with whom. For each person, enter a first name, a date and a city of birth. The time is optional.

Free

  • Your whole chart: a wheel with all ten planets, the houses and the links between them, each part read in plain words. The rising sign and the houses need the time of birth.
  • Any two people: a score out of 100, five sides of the friendship (talking, feelings, fun, energy, over time), what works and what rubs, with a tip.
  • Your sky this week: the planets passing over your chart, with their dates, and the friends going through the same.
  • Your year: from one birthday to the next, what grows and what gets built, to send as an image on the day.

The Circle Paid

  • Groups of 3 to 12: friends, roommates, family, team.
  • One role each, such as The Spark, The Rock, The Radio or The Lantern. Twelve roles in all.
  • The group's constellation and its name, drawn from who gets along best with whom: The Lighthouse of Tides, The Hearth of Embers, The Archipelago of Winds…
  • The group's map: which elements the group leans to.
  • Every pair in the group.
  • The group's week: the best evening to meet, birthdays, and a heads-up for whoever may be tired or quick to snap. Also on a home screen widget.
  • The crew's recap: five images to share.

Monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or lifetime.

How friends join

  1. Send your card as a link, or show its QR code.
  2. Your friend opens it and Stelami adds you.
  3. They send theirs back. Open the link and Stelami adds them.

No account and no server: the card travels inside the link. You can also add a friend yourself, with their date and city of birth.

Stays on your phone

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Planet positions are computed on the phone with Jean Meeus's algorithms (VSOP87). Places come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and ship inside the app.

Languages

English, Français, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch and Italiano.

Questions

Do I need my time of birth?

No. The time gives the rising sign and the houses. Without it, everything else works.

Do my friends need the app?

No. You can add a friend yourself, with their date and city of birth. If they have Stelami, they can send you their card instead.
