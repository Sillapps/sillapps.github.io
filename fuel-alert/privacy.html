Who is responsible

The data controller for Pompada is Maxime Silla Pellicer, entrepreneur individuel (France), 19 Rue des Causses, 91940 Les Ulis, France, publishing the app under the name Sillapps. Contact: contact@sillapps.com, for any question or request about your personal data.

What the app is

Pompada shows retail fuel prices published by public sources in Turkey (EPDK), Thailand (PTT OR and Bangchak) and Malaysia (the Ministry of Finance's weekly prices on data.gov.my), the latest price changes, tomorrow's prices when they are announced, and optional alerts when a price changes. It is an informational tool; it is not a government app and is not affiliated with EPDK, PTT OR, Bangchak, the Government of Malaysia, any fuel distributor or any government agency.

Data stored on your device

Pompada has no account and does not ask for your location. Your country, the provinces and fuels you follow, your alert setting, a local copy of the downloaded prices, the last prices you were notified about, the time of the last background check, whether Pompada Pro or its free trial is active, and simple usage counters (first day of use, number of days the app was opened, when a Pro reminder or rating request was last shown) are stored only on your device. If the app stops unexpectedly, a short description of the last error is stored on your device too. None of this is sent to the developer.

Network requests for prices

The app downloads prices directly from the public services of the sources, without any personal data in the requests (only a province code and dates): in Turkey, the EPDK web services at lisansws.epdk.gov.tr and apigateway.epdk.gov.tr; in Thailand, the PTT OR price service at orapiweb.pttor.com and the Bangchak price board at oil-price.bangchak.co.th; in Malaysia, the open data API at api.data.gov.my.

As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address; their processing is governed by their own policies. The developer runs no server and receives none of this traffic.

Advertising

The free version shows one banner ad on the main screen, from the second day of use. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy options. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and interactions with ads, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone, Pompada never asks for permission to track you, so the SDK does not get Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). Pompada Pro and the free Pro trial remove ads: while they are active, and on the first day of use, the app requests no ads and does not start the ads SDK.

Purchases

Pompada Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play on Android or by Apple's App Store on iPhone. Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only asks the store whether Pro is owned. On Android, the free trial is recorded on your device only. On iPhone, the trial is a free App Store purchase: Apple records it, which allows one trial per Apple ID.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro or trial), the app checks the sources in the background about once an hour on Android (on iPhone, iOS decides when background checks run) and shows notifications generated on your device. During the free trial, one notification generated on your device tells you the day before the trial ends (or on its last day, if it could not be shown earlier) that price alerts and the widget will stop. The home screen widget (Pro or trial) displays prices stored locally by the app and makes no request of its own. If you start the countdown to an announced price, the app shows an ongoing notification with a timer on Android, or a Live Activity on the Lock Screen of an iPhone, built on your device from the prices it has already downloaded; it starts again with each new announcement when you open Pompada, and you can turn it off in Settings. None of these features sends data to the developer.

Problem reports

If you choose Settings > Report a problem, the app opens your e-mail app with a message to contact@sillapps.com containing the app version, your country, provinces, fuels, alert setting, whether Pro or the trial is active, the time and prices of the last downloads, and the last error. Nothing is sent unless you send this e-mail yourself, and you can read or edit it first. E-mails you send are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and at most 2 years.

Ratings

The app may occasionally show the store's in-app review prompt (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone). Google or Apple handles any rating you give; the app does not learn whether or how you rated it.

Purposes and legal bases

Showing prices, alerts and the widget: providing the service you ask for (performance of a contract). Purchases: performance of a contract. Advertising in the free version: the developer's legitimate interest in funding the free app, and your consent where the law requires it. Problem reports and e-mails: your request, to answer you.

International transfers

Google processes advertising data, and Google or Apple process purchase data, in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses). Price requests go directly from your phone to the sources in Turkey, Thailand and Malaysia.

Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device until you clear the app storage or uninstall the app. On iPhone, it can also be part of your device backups (iCloud or computer), which you control. Data collected by Google for advertising, and by Google or Apple for purchases, is kept as described in their privacy policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (the GDPR in Europe, KVKK law no. 6698 in Turkey, the PDPA B.E. 2562 in Thailand, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 in Malaysia), you can ask to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to know how it is processed and to whom it is transferred, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw your consent. Write to contact@sillapps.com; you will get an answer within 30 days. Since the app keeps your data on your phone, most of it can be deleted directly by clearing the app storage.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: the CNIL in France (3 Place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, www.cnil.fr), the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Turkey (www.kvkk.gov.tr), the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) in Thailand (www.pdpc.or.th), or the Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP) in Malaysia (www.pdp.gov.my).

Children

Pompada is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Apakah aplikasi ini

Pompada menunjukkan harga runcit bahan api yang diterbitkan oleh sumber awam di Turki (EPDK), Thailand (PTT OR dan Bangchak) dan Malaysia (harga mingguan Kementerian Kewangan di data.gov.my), perubahan harga terkini, harga esok apabila diumumkan, dan pemberitahuan pilihan apabila harga berubah. Ia alat maklumat sahaja; ia bukan aplikasi kerajaan dan tidak bergabung dengan EPDK, PTT OR, Bangchak, Kerajaan Malaysia, mana-mana pengedar bahan api atau agensi kerajaan.

Data yang disimpan dalam peranti anda

Pompada tidak memerlukan akaun dan tidak meminta lokasi anda. Negara anda, kawasan dan bahan api yang anda ikuti, tetapan pemberitahuan, salinan tempatan harga yang dimuat turun, harga terakhir yang diberitahu kepada anda, masa semakan latar belakang terakhir, sama ada Pompada Pro atau percubaan percumanya aktif, dan pembilang penggunaan ringkas (hari pertama penggunaan, bilangan hari aplikasi dibuka, bila peringatan Pro atau permintaan penilaian terakhir ditunjukkan) disimpan dalam peranti anda sahaja. Jika aplikasi berhenti secara tiba-tiba, penerangan ringkas ralat terakhir juga disimpan dalam peranti anda. Tiada satu pun daripadanya dihantar kepada pembangun.

Permintaan rangkaian untuk harga

Aplikasi memuat turun harga terus daripada perkhidmatan awam sumber, tanpa sebarang data peribadi dalam permintaan (hanya kod wilayah dan tarikh): di Turki, perkhidmatan web EPDK di lisansws.epdk.gov.tr dan apigateway.epdk.gov.tr; di Thailand, perkhidmatan harga PTT OR di orapiweb.pttor.com dan papan harga Bangchak di oil-price.bangchak.co.th; di Malaysia, API data terbuka di api.data.gov.my.

Seperti mana-mana permintaan internet, perkhidmatan ini boleh melihat alamat IP anda; pemprosesannya tertakluk kepada dasar mereka sendiri. Pembangun tidak mempunyai pelayan dan tidak menerima trafik ini.

Pengiklanan

Versi percuma menunjukkan satu sepanduk iklan di skrin utama, mulai hari kedua penggunaan. Iklan disediakan oleh Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Jika undang-undang memerlukannya, borang persetujuan Google ditunjukkan sebelum sebarang iklan diminta, dan anda boleh menukar pilihan kemudian di Tetapan > Pilihan privasi iklan. Untuk menyediakan dan mengukur iklan, serta mencegah penipuan, Google Mobile Ads SDK boleh mengumpul dan berkongsi dengan Google maklumat seperti ID pengiklanan peranti anda, alamat IP, lokasi anggaran daripada alamat IP, dan interaksi dengan iklan, seperti yang diterangkan dalam dasar Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Anda boleh menetapkan semula atau memadam ID pengiklanan dalam tetapan peranti. Di iPhone, Pompada tidak pernah meminta kebenaran untuk menjejak anda, jadi SDK tidak mendapat pengecam pengiklanan Apple (IDFA). Pompada Pro dan percubaan Pro percuma membuang iklan: semasa ia aktif, dan pada hari pertama penggunaan, aplikasi tidak meminta iklan dan tidak memulakan SDK iklan.

Pembelian

Pompada Pro ialah pembelian dalam aplikasi sekali sahaja yang diproses oleh Google Play di Android atau App Store Apple di iPhone. Butiran pembayaran dikendalikan sepenuhnya oleh kedai; pembangun tidak menerima maklumat pembayaran anda. Aplikasi hanya bertanya kepada kedai sama ada Pro telah dimiliki. Di Android, percubaan percuma direkodkan dalam peranti anda sahaja. Di iPhone, percubaan ialah pembelian percuma di App Store: Apple merekodkannya, jadi setiap Apple ID boleh mencuba sekali sahaja.

Pemberitahuan dan widget

Jika anda menghidupkan pemberitahuan (Pro atau percubaan), aplikasi menyemak sumber di latar belakang lebih kurang sejam sekali di Android (di iPhone, iOS menentukan bila semakan latar belakang berjalan) dan menunjukkan pemberitahuan yang dijana dalam peranti anda. Semasa percubaan percuma, satu pemberitahuan yang dijana dalam peranti anda dipaparkan sehari sebelum percubaan tamat (atau pada hari terakhirnya jika tidak dapat dipaparkan lebih awal), untuk memberitahu bahawa amaran harga dan widget akan berhenti. Widget skrin utama (Pro atau percubaan) memaparkan harga yang disimpan secara tempatan oleh aplikasi dan tidak membuat sebarang permintaan sendiri. Jika anda memulakan kiraan detik ke harga yang diumumkan, aplikasi memaparkan pemberitahuan berterusan dengan pemasa di Android, atau Live Activity pada Skrin Kunci iPhone, yang dijana dalam peranti anda daripada harga yang sudah dimuat turun. Kiraan detik bermula semula pada setiap pengumuman apabila anda membuka Pompada, dan boleh dimatikan dalam Tetapan. Ciri-ciri ini tidak menghantar data kepada pembangun.

Laporan masalah

Jika anda memilih Tetapan > Laporkan masalah, aplikasi membuka aplikasi e-mel anda dengan mesej kepada contact@sillapps.com yang mengandungi versi aplikasi, negara, kawasan, bahan api, tetapan pemberitahuan, sama ada Pro atau percubaan aktif, masa dan harga muat turun terakhir, dan ralat terakhir. Tiada apa-apa dihantar melainkan anda sendiri menghantar e-mel ini, dan anda boleh membaca atau menyuntingnya dahulu. E-mel yang anda hantar disimpan hanya selama yang perlu untuk menjawab anda, dan paling lama 2 tahun.

Penilaian

Aplikasi mungkin sekali-sekala menunjukkan gesaan penilaian dalam aplikasi kedai (Google Play di Android, App Store di iPhone). Google atau Apple mengendalikan sebarang penilaian yang anda berikan; aplikasi tidak mengetahui sama ada atau bagaimana anda menilainya.

Tujuan dan asas undang-undang
