Privacy Policy

SILLAPPS

App name: Pompada (package com.sillapps.fuelalert)

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Pompada handles information when you use the mobile application. Pompada shows official retail fuel prices published by public sources in Turkey (EPDK) and Thailand (PTT OR and Bangchak), the latest price changes, and optional alerts when a price changes. Pompada is an informational tool; it is not an official app and is not affiliated with EPDK, PTT OR, Bangchak, any fuel distributor or any government agency.

Data stored on your device

Pompada has no account and does not ask for your location. Your country, the provinces and fuels you follow, your alert setting, a local copy of the downloaded prices, the last prices you were notified about, whether Pompada Pro or its free trial is active, and simple usage counters (first day of use, number of days the app was opened, when a Pro reminder or rating request was last shown) are stored only on your device. These counters are never sent to the developer; they only keep reminders rare.

Network requests for prices

The app downloads prices directly from the public services of the sources, without any personal data in the requests (only a province code and dates):

1. Turkey: EPDK (Enerji Piyasası Düzenleme Kurumu) web services at lisansws.epdk.gov.tr and apigateway.epdk.gov.tr.

2. Thailand: the PTT OR price service at orapiweb.pttor.com and the Bangchak price board at oil-price.bangchak.co.th.

As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address; their processing is governed by their own policies. The developer runs no server and receives none of this traffic.

Advertising

The free version shows one banner ad on the main screen, from the second day of use. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown where required, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy options. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interactions with ads, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Pompada Pro and the free Pro trial remove ads: while they are active, and on the first day of use, the app requests no ads and does not start the ads SDK.

Purchases

Pompada Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; the developer does not receive your payment information. The app only checks with the store whether Pro is owned. The free trial is recorded on your device only.

Notifications and widget

If you enable alerts (Pro or trial), the app checks the sources in the background about once an hour and shows notifications generated on your device. The home screen widget displays prices stored locally by the app. Neither feature sends data to the developer.

Ratings

The app may occasionally show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google handles any rating you give; the app does not learn whether or how you rated it.

Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children's privacy

Pompada is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
