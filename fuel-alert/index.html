Three countries, every province

Türkiye

  • Petrol (95), diesel (motorin) and LPG (autogas) in all 81 provinces, İstanbul Asian and European sides separately.
  • Province price = median of the prices distributors declared, with the range, the cheapest distributor and the full list.
  • Tomorrow's price, labelled "Yarın", once distributors declare it; last change and a 30-day chart.

Thailand

  • Gasohol 95, 91, E20, E85 (Bangkok), diesel B7, B20, premium diesel and benzine 95.
  • Bangkok and every other province, with the price in each district.
  • Tomorrow's prices once a change is announced in the evening, effective from 05:00.

Malaysia

  • RON95 without subsidy, RON95 BUDI95 (subsidised price for eligible Malaysians), RON97 and diesel.
  • Peninsular Malaysia, and Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan (own diesel price).
  • Next week's prices as soon as they are published on Wednesday evening, effective Thursday at 00:01.

Sources

EPDK web services (Türkiye), PTT OR and Bangchak prices (Thailand), data.gov.my fuel prices of the Ministry of Finance Malaysia (CC BY 4.0).

Province prices in Türkiye are medians computed by Pompada from the distributors' declarations; outside Bangkok, tomorrow's price is today's provincial price plus the announced change. These sources publish no LPG or NGV prices for Thailand. Station prices can differ slightly. Pompada never predicts price hikes.

Pompada is for information only. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with EPDK, PTT OR, Bangchak, the Government of Malaysia, the Ministry of Finance, any fuel distributor or government agency.

FAQ

Where do the prices come from?

From public price services: EPDK in Türkiye, PTT OR and Bangchak in Thailand, and data.gov.my in Malaysia. Every screen shows the source, the download time and a link.

When does tomorrow's price appear?

As soon as it is announced: in Türkiye when distributors declare it to EPDK, in Thailand with the evening announcement (effective 05:00), in Malaysia on Wednesday evening (effective Thursday at 00:01).

Does Pompada predict price hikes?

No. It only shows prices published by the sources, never guesses.

Do I need an account or to share my location?

No. There is no account and no location permission: you pick your province, and your settings stay on your phone.
