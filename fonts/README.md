# Fonts

Fonts of the Sillapps apps, converted from the `assets/fonts/*.ttf` of each app repo to
subset `woff2` (Latin + Latin Extended-A) with fontTools, and declared in `fonts.css`.
They are served from sillapps.com on purpose: no Google Fonts or other CDN, so no visitor
IP address is sent to a third party (GDPR).

All fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: Manrope, Space Grotesk, Saira (and Saira at width 112.5, "Saira Wide"),
Barlow Semi Condensed, Anek Latin, Audiowide, Inter, Oxanium, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (Hora Barata, official IBM files, `OFL-IBMPlex.txt`), Nunito Sans (Dia do Pagamento, `OFL-NunitoSans.txt`).
