# Fonts

Fonts of the Sillapps apps, converted from the `assets/fonts/*.ttf` of each app repo to
subset `woff2` (Latin + Latin Extended-A) with fontTools, and declared in `fonts.css`.
They are served from sillapps.com on purpose: no Google Fonts or other CDN, so no visitor
IP address is sent to a third party (GDPR).

All fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: Manrope, Space Grotesk, Saira (and Saira at width 112.5, "Saira Wide"),
Barlow Semi Condensed, Anek Latin, Audiowide, Inter, Oxanium, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (Hora Barata, official IBM files, `OFL-IBMPlex.txt`), Work Sans and Abril Fatface (Dia do Pagamento, `OFL-WorkSans.txt`, `OFL-AbrilFatface.txt`), Chivo (Pompada, `OFL-Chivo.txt`), Lexend (BioWetter Kompass, `OFL-Lexend.txt`), Fira Sans and Fira Sans Condensed (Scioperi Oggi, `OFL-FiraSans.txt`), Fraunces and Figtree (Stelami, `OFL-Fraunces.txt`, `OFL-Figtree.txt`), Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans (Cactolo, `OFL-BricolageGrotesque.txt`, `OFL-DMSans.txt`; kept variable: Bricolage pinned at width 92 as in the app, weight and optical size free).
