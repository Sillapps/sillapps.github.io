NEON OVERLOAD

Privacy Policy

App name: Neon Overload

Developer: Sillapps

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Overload handles information when you use the mobile application. Neon Overload is a mobile game with local progression, an optional Google Play Games leaderboard, optional in-app purchases, optional rewarded ads served through Google AdMob, and advertising privacy consent served through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Who is responsible

The data controller for Neon Overload is Maxime Silla Pellicer, who publishes the app as Sillapps. You can reach the controller at contact@sillapps.com for any question or request about your personal data.

Data collected

Game data stored on your device. Neon Overload saves your progression locally: stars and best scores per level, credits, unlocked talents, purchased items, tutorial progress, language, audio, vibration and screen-shake settings. Neon Overload does not require an account and does not operate a first-party server or analytics backend.

Android backup. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy this game save to your Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or change phones. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Google Play Games. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses your Play Games player profile (player ID and display name, as provided by Google) to submit your best scores to public leaderboards. Your display name and scores are visible to other players on those leaderboards. Signing in is optional; you can play without it.

Advertising, consent and purchases. Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google Play Billing may collect and process limited technical data needed to operate those services, such as device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction events, diagnostics, performance information, transaction status, and fraud-prevention or security signals.

Crash reports. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive a report when it crashes or hits an unexpected error: the error details and stack trace, the app version, device model, Android version, and a random Crashlytics installation identifier. No advertising identifier is included.

Gameplay statistics. Where your consent is required, and only if you accept it in the privacy form, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a few gameplay events (level started or finished with the wave reached, tutorial completed or skipped, rewarded ad watched, talent unlocked), together with a random app-instance identifier and basic device information. Elsewhere these statistics are collected without a prompt. They never include your advertising identifier and are not used for advertising.

Reviews and updates. The app uses Google Play's in-app review and in-app update features. These are provided by Google Play; Neon Overload does not receive your rating or review through them.

Feedback form. The optional "Send feedback" entry opens a Google Forms questionnaire. Only what you choose to type in it is collected, and it is processed by Google Forms on behalf of the developer.

How data is used and legal bases

Local game data is used to save your game state and preferences between sessions. This is necessary to provide the game you asked to use (performance of a contract).

Leaderboard submissions are used to rank your best scores against other players when you choose to sign in to Google Play Games (performance of a contract).

Purchase data is used by Google Play to process purchases, restore entitlements, and prevent fraud (performance of a contract and legal obligations).

Advertising data is used to deliver the rewarded ads you choose to watch, record and apply your privacy choices, measure ad performance, and prevent abuse. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), personalized ads are only requested with your consent; if you decline, the app requests non-personalized ads (consent, and legitimate interest in funding the free game for non-personalized ads).

Crash reports are used to find and fix bugs (legitimate interest in keeping the game working). Gameplay statistics are used to see where players get stuck and improve the game (consent where required by law, otherwise legitimate interest).

Feedback you send is used to answer you and improve the game (legitimate interest).

Third-party services

Neon Overload relies on Google services: Google AdMob (advertising), Google User Messaging Platform (consent), Google Play Billing (purchases), Google Play Games Services (leaderboards), Google Play in-app review and in-app update, Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google Analytics for Firebase (gameplay statistics), and Google Forms (feedback). Their processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards Google provides, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your privacy choices and rights

Where required by law, Neon Overload asks for your advertising privacy choices before requesting any ad. You can review or change them at any time from the in-app Privacy Settings button, which also lets you withdraw consent.

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing done before. To exercise these rights, contact contact@sillapps.com. Because game data is stored on your device, you can also delete it directly (see Data retention). For data held by Google (Play Games profile, advertising data, Android backup), you can use your Google account settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France (3 Place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, www.cnil.fr) or the authority of your country of residence.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in your device settings. An Android backup is kept by Google according to your Google account settings.

Leaderboard scores stay on Google Play Games leaderboards until you delete your Play Games profile data. Crash reports are kept for 90 days and gameplay statistics for 2 months, then deleted. Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to handle them.

Other technical logs generated inside the app stay on the device.

Security

Neon Overload keeps core progression data on the device and minimizes first-party data handling. However, no device, network, or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

Neon Overload is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app is not configured as a child-directed service under COPPA, and ads are limited to content rated for teens. Neon Overload does not knowingly collect personal data from children through a first-party account system or direct submission forms.

Changes to this privacy policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, data handling, or legal requirements change. Any update will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
