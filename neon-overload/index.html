Five towers, five ways to play

  • Pulse — chain lightning that arcs between enemies. The reliable all-rounder.
  • Photon — a continuous laser that melts bosses.
  • Nova — area shockwaves for dense waves and swarms.
  • Cryo — stasis fields that slow and freeze.
  • Pylon — a support tower that links to a neighbor and supercharges it (in-app purchase).

What's inside

  • 16 handcrafted maps with 30 waves each, and a Heroic mode for every map.
  • Fifty permanent upgrades across six skill trees, with a specialization per tower.
  • Overcharges to clear the board when a wave is about to break through.
  • Act 4 terrain: slow zones, speed lanes and power tiles.
  • Global leaderboards when you're online.
  • Quick runs of 5 to 15 minutes.

FAQ

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole campaign plays without wifi or mobile data. Leaderboards simply sync the next time you're online.

Are there forced ads?

No. There are no banners and no interstitials. Ads are optional videos you choose to watch for a bonus reward.

Do I have to pay?

No. Every level can be beaten for free. Purchases remove ads, speed up progression, and unlock cosmetics and the Pylon support tower.

Which devices are supported?

Android 9 and later, on phones and tablets.
